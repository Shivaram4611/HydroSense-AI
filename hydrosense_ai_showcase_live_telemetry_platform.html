Layer 01: Edge Sensing

IoT Sensor Array

Industrial submersible probes measuring pH, Electrical Conductivity, Turbidity, ORP, and Heavy Metal optical density in continuous high-frequency flow.

Protocol: Modbus RS485 / ADC
Layer 02: Telemetry

Edge Telemetry Hub

Ultra-low-power ESP32 & STM32 gateways aggregating signals with edge noise filtering. Dispatches encrypted packets via LoRaWAN (rural) or 4G LTE/MQTT (metro).

Stack: LoRaWAN 868MHz / TLS MQTT

1. Executive Summary & Vision

Contaminated drinking water distribution networks remain a primary vector for preventable waterborne illnesses worldwide. Conventional reliance on periodic manual sampling is fundamentally slow, leaving multi-day latency gaps where pathogens disperse before discovery. HydroSense AI deploys distributed edge-sensing nodes combined with predictive machine learning algorithms across water distribution pipes, providing instant contamination warnings and proactive structural maintenance.

2. Core Innovations & Architecture

Unlike static point-of-use filters, the platform functions at the municipal infrastructure level. By synthesizing optical turbidity, electrochemical pH, heavy metal trace indicators, and hydraulic pressure, our model distinguishes between harmless mineral changes and dangerous biological or chemical contamination.

3. Alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goals

• UN SDG 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation - Target 6.1 & 6.3): Accelerates universal access to safe water and reduces untreated discharge pollution.
• UN SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being): Proactively interrupts the transmission chain of waterborne pathogens like cholera, typhoid, and dysentery.
• UN SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure): Modernizes legacy municipal water grids with digital twin capabilities.
